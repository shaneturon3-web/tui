[RULING — 2026-11-04 — VERIFY CONSOLIDATIONS BY SUBTRACTING THE ADDITION]
Ordered by Shane, 2026-11-04 (correction of method).
When a consolidated version declares an addition (legend/header), verify by subtracting the added part and hashing the remaining body against the manifest's body hash. A different full-file hash is not by itself a discrepancy when the addition is declared.
Precedent: Genesis Seed V5.3 — body minus legend hashes to the manifest body_sha256.
