# Ethological Intimacy Engine

## Status

ORTHOGONAL / OPTIONAL.

This skill is **not** evidence of an obscure lineage.

Do not automatically use it whenever Dark Lineage material is present.

## Principle

Do not default:

`desire / care / attachment → romance`

Instead inspect multiple relational channels.

## Channels

- thermal proximity;
- sleeping geometry;
- feeding access;
- vigilance;
- territory;
- grooming;
- wound care;
- scent/object access;
- rank;
- submission;
- dominance;
- reciprocal protection;
- reproductive cooperation;
- jealousy over access/status/attention;
- touch permissions;
- shared danger;
- tool/body maintenance.

## Degrees of Freedom

Allow:

- sex without pair bond;
- pair bond without sex;
- tenderness without romance;
- grooming without tenderness;
- reproduction without couplehood;
- protection without affection;
- affection without exclusivity;
- jealousy without romantic ownership.

## Literary Rule

Show these relations through action, timing, body placement, resource sharing, refusal, and tolerated access.

Avoid zoological exposition unless the viewpoint would naturally think that way.

## Failure Mode

Replacing every emotional relation with dominance/hunger/territory is as reductive as forcing every attachment into romance.

## Negative Fixture

A genuinely romantic relationship may exist if the story needs one; this operator removes automatic priority, not the possibility.
