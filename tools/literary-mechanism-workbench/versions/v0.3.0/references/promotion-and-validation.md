# Promotion and validation

## Separate two tracks

**Narrative mechanism:** a relation that can produce distinct story pressures, choices, or consequences.  
**Skill:** an operational behavior with a trigger, inputs, prerequisites, decisions, outputs, limits, recovery path, and tests.

A mechanism can inform a skill; a named mechanism does not itself make a skill.

## Candidate → validated

Promote only when the candidate has:

1. direct source anchors and a declared evidence class;
2. a distinct decision delta (what an operator does differently);
3. activation and non-activation conditions;
4. at least one limit, counterexample, or adversarial test;
5. an independent transfer case, with diagnostic understanding separated from execution quality;
6. no systematic bias toward preserving source relics, erasing materiality, closing uncertainty, or overfitting the source family.

When the evidence set is paired text, use leave-one-pair-out where feasible. Withhold one target; score diagnosis and execution separately; reveal the target only after the prediction is recorded. For source supports, record precision and recall separately, then inspect whether substituted supports perform the same function.

## Do not conflate

- number of mentions with independent occurrences;
- a design note with a narrative execution;
- a finished story with evidence of how it was transformed;
- a plausible interpretation with a source fact;
- a locally complete causal chain with a correct montage decision;
- procedural conformity with literary quality.

## Stop and report

Stop a pass when new items only restate known families, source coverage is bounded, or an unresolved distinction changes the next sampling choice. Report the coverage boundary, missing sources, unresolved claims, and the next discriminating test. No promotion by elegant naming or aggregate score alone.
