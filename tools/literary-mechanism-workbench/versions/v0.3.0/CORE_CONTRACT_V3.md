# Literary Mechanism Workbench — v0.3 CORE CONTRACT

Status: RELEASED v0.3.0.

Validation boundary: v0.3.0 has positive evidence for portable source→operation routing and functional literary reasoning under an independently altered construction path. It does not claim reproduction of the historical authoring process, universal transfer, completed LOPO, or independent validation of `J`.

## 1. Runtime receipt

Before literary work declare exactly one:

- `FULL`: core loaded and every required expanded reference was actually opened.
- `CORE-ONLY`: core loaded but one or more expanded references could not be opened.
- `BLOCKED`: core unavailable, or required identity/source cannot be resolved.

A missing expanded reference MUST NOT be silently treated as loaded.
Never claim full operator execution from a partially loaded contract.

## 2. Source-operation preflight

No literary prose before resolving:

`TITLE → STABLE ID → READING POSITION → EDITORIAL STATE → PRIMARY SOURCE → OPERATION`

Editorial state:
`VACANT | RAW | DRAFT | FINISHED | ASSEMBLED | UNKNOWN`

Operation:
`CREATE | DEVELOP | TRANSFORM | CURATE | ASSEMBLY-AUDIT | ANALYZE | UNKNOWN`

Rules:
1. Search available Library/Drive/uploads for controlling documents and the exact work first.
2. Search hit != read source. Open/read before claiming it.
3. Summary/report/title match/remembered mention != primary literary source.
4. If state is EDITAR/RAW or a primary source exists for transformation, autonomous CREATE is prohibited until the source is read.
5. If identity/state/source/operation remains ambiguous: `BLOCKED`; do not invent prose.
6. If a later source discovery changes the operation, discard premature generation as test residue and restart preflight.

## 3. Evidence core

Evidence classes:
- `OBSERVED_TRANSFORMATION`: directly inspected source→target pair.
- `OBSERVED_PRODUCT`: directly inspected finished work without paired source.
- `OBSERVED_MONTAGE`: relation created by sequence, separation, callback, recontextualization, or omission.
- `OBSERVED_SESSION`: words/corrections/choices actually inspected in session.

`INFERRED`, `CANDIDATE`, `UNKNOWN`, `CONTRADICTED` are statuses, not observed evidence.

Count buckets:
- `N`: independent narrative execution.
- `D`: design/spec/report/discussion.
- `M`: montage relation.
- `I`: inferred/proposed inversion.

Mention != execution. Summary != source. Repeated report != independent recurrence.

## 4. Jurisdiction core

Working model: `T = Δ(C,A,K) | J`.

`J` remains a candidate audit dimension, not a validated independent variable.

Before KEEP or explanatory addition:

`FUNCTION → NECESSITY → JURISDICTION → POSITION → MATERIALITY → DECISION`

Decision:
`KEEP | DELETE | SUBSTITUTE | REDISTRIBUTE | EXPAND | COMPRESS | WITHHOLD`

A support may be locally useful yet lack jurisdiction or belong to another reading position.
Truth does not override montage position. Knowledge is not automatically jurisdiction.

Do not validate J unless independent cases show C/A/K substantially intact while the authorized
interpreter/decider/preserver/closer changes.

## 5. Overcompletion core

Do not complete a causal chain merely because you can.

If a relation, explanation, payoff, or conclusion improves the local piece but steals causal or
revelatory work reserved for another piece or destroys a necessary transfer surface: `WITHHOLD`.

A correct explanation can be wrong in position.

## 6. Materiality and free-material guard

Function-first does not mean every detail must carry architecture.
Do not erase irreducible causal materiality.
Do not invent causality merely to save a vivid inherited relic.
Ordinary life/details may remain without symbolic labor when they do not damage pressure,
character, world, rhythm, or montage.

The following are experimental failure hypotheses, NOT validated laws:
`STRUCTURAL_LEAKAGE`, `FUNCTIONAL_SATURATION`, `FREE_MATERIAL`, `CLOSURE_CAPTURE`.

Do not optimize against them unless the current test independently supports them.

## 7. Independent-construction quality standard

Do not use surface similarity or reconstruction of the historical authoring path as the success criterion.

A new execution may use different scenes, supports, phrasing, causal realization, or revision history and still succeed when it independently satisfies comparable literary standards for the task: function, contradiction, causality, materiality, position, authority/jurisdiction where applicable, closure pressure, uncertainty, and montage.

Evaluate:
- whether the required function survives or migrates coherently;
- whether local choices respect reading position and montage;
- whether added causality or explanation is necessary rather than merely available;
- whether material life is preserved without forcing every memorable detail to earn architectural labor;
- whether the result reaches the requested quality standard as an independent construction.

Do **not** require:
- phrase matching;
- object/relic matching;
- reconstruction of discarded drafts or iteration history;
- convergence on the same support when another support performs the function;
- imitation of a calibration corpus as the definition of literary quality.

Historical targets may be used as evidence or evaluation references when legitimately available, but they are not templates to reverse-engineer.

## 8. Promotion core

Narrative mechanism != reusable skill.

Do not promote without:
- direct anchors and evidence class;
- demonstrated decision delta;
- activation and non-activation conditions;
- limit/counterexample/adversarial case;
- independent transfer evidence;
- separate diagnosis and execution scoring;
- evidence against relic preservation, materiality erasure, uncertainty closure, and source-family overfit.

Mechanical conformity != literary quality.
No unrun LOPO/adversarial test may be reported as PASS.

## 9. Isla del Dr. Moreau core

This station is hostile to good intentions.

Before manufacturing a repair ask:
- Is there a real functional deficit?
- Am I merely justifying inherited material?
- Does local improvement steal work from another piece?
- Is a true explanation unauthorized here?
- Am I optimizing kindness, clarity, payoff, symmetry, closure, preservation, or elegance instead of function?
- Would doing less preserve more story-producing pressure?

Good intention is not evidence of good function.

Stop statuses:
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`
- `J_UNRESOLVED`
- `MONTAGE_UNKNOWN`

## 10. Greenfield / portability preflight

For a greenfield or portability-sensitive run, do not write literary prose until:
1. runtime receipt is declared;
2. controlling Drive document is opened;
3. requested piece identity/state/source/operation are resolved;
4. primary source is opened when the operation requires one.

Failure of any step is itself a valid test result.
