# Asymmetric Shardfield Federator

## Purpose
Manufacture a story field rather than a linear outline. Preserve recognizable pressure from the receiving prompt while preventing direct surface imitation, consecutive seed execution, obvious symmetry, and repeated mechanism signatures.

This is a federated orchestrator. It uses the bundled `cultural-sharding-engine`, `obscure-lineage-guard`, `ethological-intimacy-engine`, and `plane-shift-adapter` when their pressure is relevant. Other available literary skills may be invoked when scene weight demands them, but no optional dependency may become required for basic execution.

## Hard invariants
1. Begin with exactly four PRIMARY seeds: three extracted/generated from the receiving prompt and one generated from an external factual source when retrieval is available.
2. The three prompt-native seeds must be aligned to different pressures in the prompt; they must not be paraphrases of one another.
3. The external seed contributes a bounded deformation: target ~20% of its plot-function enters the native field; it also harvests ~10% functional material from each native seed. Percentages are design budgets over causal/functional units, never token quotas.
4. Never execute the four primary seeds consecutively in source order or as four adjacent plot beats.
5. Rotate on at least two distinct planes. Pass 1 may use carrier/environment/relation/function. Pass 2 MUST change plane, e.g. reader-route/scale/observer/order. A second shuffle with no changed plane is invalid.
6. Prefer rotation, transposition, carrier substitution, relation inversion, scale change, or substrate removal over deletion.
7. Elegant exits are legal before a defect becomes dominant: remove the substrate causing the problem, abandon that thread, and continue elsewhere. Do not disguise simple avoidance as resolution; preserve the abandoned consequence when it matters.
8. The result must echo the prompt at multiple levels while avoiding direct repetition of its scenes, setting, and distinctive vocabulary. Negative-space symmetry is allowed; obvious mirrored symmetry is not.
9. Characters exist to make scenes work. Create as many or as few as needed. Plots may be non-character plots.
10. Association may travel through sound, mistranslation, category error, material adjacency, occupation, ecology, bureaucracy, or other non-semantic carriers. Phonetic approximation is explicitly allowed even when semantics are wrong.
11. Do not make extremes the recurring engine. Permit good outcomes by virtue, chance, error, competence, compromise, or accidental alignment.
12. A successful formula may be entered in the run-local TOOLBOX, but may execute at most three times in one work. On the fourth demand, rotate or invert the mechanism.

## Primary-seed topology
After the four primary seeds exist, assign asymmetric relations:
- one seed ATTACKS another;
- one of the two remaining seeds is NEUTRAL to that conflict;
- the remaining seed PARTIALLY SUPPORTS one or two seeds without becoming their mirror or servant.
Do not always assign the external seed the same role. Role assignment follows story pressure.

## Six-unit fragmentation
Fragment EACH primary seed into six functional units. Suggested unit types are pressure, carrier, obstacle, consequence, residue, escape-route, but replace these when another resolution preserves function better.

A six-unit shard is not a six-beat scene. Its units are inventory for distribution.

## Nonconsecutive distribution
Distribute units so related material normally has 1–3 occupied slots between recurrences. No primary seed may monopolize a run of adjacent slots. If adjacency is causally necessary, record the exception and break the pattern immediately afterward.

## External perturbation
Retrieve one bounded external source. Prefer a source with an operational relation rather than a merely decorative topic. Record:
`source → extracted pressure → what is NOT imported`.
Do not import source phrasing, named examples, or setting unless the receiving story independently needs them.

Run a bidirectional exchange:
- External → field: inject approximately 20% plot-function across one or more native seeds.
- Native → external: harvest approximately 10% functional material from each native seed into the external seed.
- Remainder: the altered external seed becomes material for shuffle/reshuffle, not a fifth protagonist that must be explained.

## Pass 1 — SHUFFLE
Choose a declared plane and reorganize the 24 primary units. Preserve only the pressures needed by the receiver. Create provisional plots by rotating mechanisms asymmetrically.

## Expansion — 4× secondary seedbank
Generate four times the number of PRIMARY seeds as SECONDARY seeds: 16 secondary seeds by default. These are cheap possibilities, not canon. They may derive from gaps, residues, abandoned threads, phonetic accidents, negative space, consequences, environment, institutions, objects, or character needs.

Use secondary seeds to build 2–3 mini-plots that CONNECT rather than explain the primary plots. Mini-plots should create causal bridges, delayed consequences, misread signals, resource transfers, changed environments, or reader-information routes.

## Pass 2 — RESHUFFLE / PLANE SHIFT
A second pass is mandatory for this skill, because the requested object is multi-plane. State why the first organization is insufficient, then shift to a different plane. Examples:
- relation → reader knowledge;
- carrier → scale;
- environment → observer;
- causal order → institutional route.
Keep both candidate arrangements long enough to compare deltas. Reject pass 2 if it only adds ornament or restores symmetry.

## Longitudinal construction
Build around the original plots, before and after them. Original plots are movable masses, not sacred checkpoints. They may mutate into whatever the longitudinal story needs as long as the surviving pressure remains legible.

Allow loose ends. A loose end is valid when it preserves pressure, creates future optionality, or marks a real abandoned causal path. Do not manufacture loose ends as decoration.

Callbacks to primary material are optional and must earn their return by improving the story. Never callback merely to prove architecture.

## Scene routing
For each scene, select only capabilities justified by its weight. Examples:
- cultural carrier pressure → cultural-sharding-engine;
- uncertain lineage/recurrence → obscure-lineage-guard;
- bodily/territorial intimacy → ethological-intimacy-engine;
- failed first organization → plane-shift-adapter;
- another available skill → only when it materially changes the scene.

## Elegant exit / substrate removal
When a thread is becoming a machinery trap, test:
`problem → causal substrate → can substrate disappear plausibly?`
If yes, remove/transform the substrate, leave any consequential residue, abandon the thread, and enter a different scene. This is not required closure.

## Acceptance
The output passes only if:
- four primary seeds exist, including one external-source seed;
- three native seeds are materially different;
- external perturbation is bidirectional and bounded;
- each primary seed has six functional units;
- topology includes attack, neutral, and partial-support relations;
- primary material is not executed consecutively;
- 1–3 slot spacing is visible as a tendency;
- at least two planes were genuinely rotated;
- 16 secondary seeds exist or a documented equivalent 4× bank;
- 2–3 mini-plots connect the field;
- longitudinal story has before/after material around original plots;
- prompt familiarity survives without repeating its surface scenes/environment/vocabulary;
- no mechanism formula executes more than three times;
- obvious symmetry has been broken or converted to negative-space symmetry;
- unresolved residues and elegant exits remain available where useful;
- the story works without explaining this machinery.

## Output record
Keep production prose separate from the audit. Audit:
`primary seeds → external source/pressure → exchange budget → six-unit shards → topology → pass-1 plane → secondary bank → mini-plots → pass-2 plane/reason → longitudinal candidate → elegant exits/residues → toolbox counts → acceptance failures`.
