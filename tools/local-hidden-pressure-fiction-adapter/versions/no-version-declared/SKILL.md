

# Hidden Pressure Fiction Adapter

A thin adapter between analytical operators and prose. It adds **no new mechanism**. Its job is only:

`operator → hidden pressure → embodiment → prose → anti-doctrine revision`

Preserve the source mechanism without naming it in the story.

## Operator routing

Use existing operators according to phase rather than invoking all of them at once:

- **First draft / generation:** `preserve-function-not-artifact` and `route-contradiction-before-resolution` are the preferred upstream pressures when applicable.
- **Quarry / intake:** `capture-before-organization` belongs before this adapter, not inside prose generation.
- **Revision / selection:** `selection-as-irreversible-cut` belongs primarily after a draft exists. Do not let it make first-draft prose visibly engineered.

If those operators are unavailable, accept equivalent abstract pressures from the user or source. Do not invent missing doctrine merely to satisfy this workflow.

## Five gates

### 1. Scene-first

Before foregrounding any mechanism, verify that the scene can live for roughly **200–400 words as a scene**: somebody wants, notices, avoids, carries, eats, fixes, waits, lies, misremembers, interrupts, leaves, returns, or otherwise does something that matters locally.

Fail if the scene exists only to demonstrate a mechanism.

### 2. Hidden-pressure translation

Before writing a sentence of mechanism into prose, translate every abstract pressure into physical or social evidence.

Preferred embodiments include:

- position or displacement of objects;
- gesture, hesitation, habit, interruption, or touch;
- a wrong word or disputed category;
- memory mismatch or temporal mismatch;
- material action, wear, repair, residue, route, or sequence;
- who observes whom and how observation changes behavior;
- domestic, bodily, procedural, or relational consequence.

Do not put analytical terms such as `provenance`, `carrier`, `route`, `contradiction`, `selection`, `invariant`, `mechanism`, or their disguised definitions into the prose unless the scene independently requires the ordinary word.

The reader may infer the pressure. The narrator must not lecture it.

### 3. Uncertainty diversity

Do not let all instability collapse into one causal mystery such as “who did it?” or “what really happened?”

When ambiguity is part of the assignment, distribute it across genuinely different dimensions where the seed supports them: kinship, category, provenance, temporal memory, institutional classification, observer effect, ownership, role, sequence, or motive.

Fail if every uncertainty is merely another accessory to the same hidden cause.

Do not manufacture ambiguity unsupported by the scene. Facts may remain solid while classification or relation remains unstable.

### 4. Worth gate

Mentally remove the mechanism. Something human should still remain worth remembering: humor, irritation, affection, appetite, embarrassment, tenderness, competence, pettiness, fatigue, awkwardness, grief, desire, or another lived pressure.

Fail if removing the mechanism leaves only a clever construction.

Worth is not sentimentality. A scene can be dry, ugly, comic, procedural, or uncomfortable and still have human worth.

### 5. Anti-explanation

During revision, inspect especially the last paragraph and every callback.

Cut or reincarnate any sentence that:

- summarizes the lesson;
- explains what an object “really represented”;
- decodes a callback for the reader;
- declares which interpretation was correct when the story does not require a verdict;
- converts uncertainty into authorial withholding followed by a reveal.

Prefer action, changed placement, repeated behavior, consequence, silence, or an ordinary material fact over explanation.

A callback must not require recognition to make the story complete.

## Budgets, not mechanisms

**Semantic names** are an optional budget. Preserve them when supplied or when the story already supports them, but do not treat semantic naming as a mechanism that must appear.

**Carrier ambiguity** is not promoted here as an independent mechanism. Treat it as a possible effect of existing pressures until it survives independent transfer testing.

## Integrity constraints

- Honest misdirection: attention may be wrong; facts must not be falsified merely to manufacture a twist.
- Consequence without verdict: unresolved interpretation does not mean nothing happens. Choices still cost, change, preserve, damage, or leave residue.
- Rotated provenance: source scenes may donate pressure, timing, sensory truth, or object behavior without donating identity, biography, causal chain, or outcome by default.
- No automatic canon promotion. Generated prose remains draft unless the user explicitly promotes it.
- Preserve native oddities that carry function. Do not normalize a strange word, object, or relation merely because a conventional version reads more smoothly.

## Output behavior

For generation requests, return the fiction first. Do not append a mechanism explanation unless requested.

For revision requests, revise minimally and preserve functioning ambiguity. If a gate fails and cannot be repaired without changing the story's identity, state the failing gate briefly instead of silently replacing the story.

For comparison/testing requests, report only the gate deltas that materially distinguish the variants.
