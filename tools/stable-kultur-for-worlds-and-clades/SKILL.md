# Stable Kultur for Worlds and Clades

## Function

Create a stable Kultur for a world, species, clade, lineage or interdependent
ecology of agents.

The skill does not create a decorative customs list. It manufactures the
shared operating culture that lets members:

- recognize one another;
- act without routing every ordinary decision;
- distinguish common competence from specialized judgment;
- preserve identity while changing form;
- interpret signals, refusals, silence and counter-moves;
- survive contact with other clades or incompatible ontologies;
- transmit obligations without making them absolute destiny.

## Stability principle

```text
STABLE KULTUR = preserved invariants under authorized variation
```

Stable does not mean static, homogeneous, consensual or morally good. A Kultur
is stable when its members can still recognize the relations that make them a
collective after mutation, migration, technological change, translation,
hybridization, schism or environmental pressure.

The primary object is not the surface custom. It is the relation that survives
or fails beneath the custom.

## Source distinctions

Preserve these distinctions from Die Kultur:

```text
KULTUR      = shared character, epistemics and universal operating literacy
PROFESSION  = specialized judgment activated when it changes the work
SKILL       = callable capability
RUNTIME     = body, tools, constraints and local mode of operation
PROJECT     = local rules that must not silently become world canon
```

Do not put every skill into Kultur. Do not empty Kultur until members become
helpless without specialists. The boundary is judgment: common competence is
shared; professional method remains differentiated.

## Albur contribution

Kultur is also a recognition ecology. A rule is not stable merely because it
is written. It is stable when members can recognize its operation, respond to
its violation, refuse it, reverse it or expose who imposed it.

Use the Albur displacement model:

```text
surface signal S
  → operation O
  → re-read surface S'
  → situated code K
  → public reading P1 / situated reading P2
  → recognition R
  → response, refusal, silence or counter-move C
  → positional delta Δσ
```

For a world or clade, this maps how a greeting, taboo, name, gesture,
inheritance, technological act or ecological exchange changes position.

Do not reduce Kultur to hidden meaning. Formal operation, situated code,
recognition, response and consequence remain separate variables.

## Scale model

Build at the requested resolution. Do not force every scale into the same
template.

```text
WORLD
  shared conditions, history, ecology, migration and contact pressures

CLADE
  inherited or convergent operating pattern within the world

LINEAGE / SUBCLADE
  transmission, mutation, memory and local obligation

BODY / AGENT
  actual runtime, competence, temperament and deviation

SCENE / EVENT
  pressure test where Kultur becomes observable
```

A world can contain incompatible Kulturs. A clade can carry several local
versions. A body can belong formally while failing recognition in practice.
Do not collapse these into one “society.”

## The Kultur kernel

Every generated Kultur must define a small kernel of invariants. Start with
three to seven; expand only when a test proves the addition changes a decision.

Possible invariant families:

1. **Recognition** — how a member is identified as a member.
2. **Evidence** — what counts as real, remembered, witnessed or proven.
3. **Repair** — what must happen after damage or contradiction.
4. **Boundary** — what cannot be shared, translated or crossed without cost.
5. **Exchange** — how obligations, resources, signals or care circulate.
6. **Continuity** — what must survive across generations or transformations.
7. **Refusal** — how a member can say no without becoming nonexistent.
8. **Variation** — which differences are tolerated, useful or dangerous.
9. **Death / succession** — how absence changes the collective.
10. **Future** — what the Kultur protects without pretending to predict it.

An invariant must be phrased as a relation or operation, not as a slogan.

Weak: “They value memory.”  
Strong: “A claim without a witness may guide action, but cannot close a debt.”

## Construction protocol

### 1. Establish the container

Record:

- world or clade name;
- ecological and technological conditions;
- historical depth;
- contact with other clades;
- available carriers of transmission;
- current rupture or pressure;
- what the Kultur must allow members to do.

Do not infer ethos from a name or aesthetic alone.

### 2. Separate inherited, chosen and imposed material

For each cultural feature mark:

```text
INHERITED   transmitted from ancestors or previous bodies
CHOSEN      deliberately adopted or maintained
IMPOSED     attached by conqueror, institution, ecology or outsider
EMERGENT    produced by repeated interaction without central design
UNKNOWN     unresolved
```

The same practice may have different origins for different subclades.

### 3. Manufacture the kernel

Write the minimum invariant set. For each invariant specify:

- ordinary use;
- carrier;
- recognition signal;
- violation;
- repair;
- permitted mutation;
- point where the invariant ceases to be the same invariant.

### 4. Add differentiated competence

Create the ordinary competence every member needs. Then create professions,
castes, guilds, bodies, symbionts or specialist runtimes only where judgment
materially differs.

```text
common competence → specialist threshold → professional method
```

Do not make a clade culturally coherent by making every member identical.

### 5. Build recognition topology

Map at least:

```text
member → target | witnesses | institution | outsider
```

For each major signal, ask:

- who recognizes the public surface;
- who reconstructs the situated reading;
- who recognizes the violation first;
- who can answer;
- who may refuse;
- who controls the official record;
- what happens when recognition is distributed unevenly.

### 6. Generate adversarial variations

For every invariant, create:

- a faithful enactment;
- a competent mutation;
- a failed recognition;
- an imposed counterfeit;
- a reversal from below;
- a case where the invariant survives under a new carrier;
- a case where the surface survives but the function is gone.

This prevents “culture” from becoming a single explanatory key.

### 7. Test across pressure

Run the Kultur through:

- migration;
- translation;
- interclade contact;
- technological substitution;
- ecological collapse;
- reproduction or succession;
- memory loss;
- schism;
- hybrid body;
- hostile misdescription by an outsider.

At each pressure, identify what remains, what changes and what is falsely
declared continuous.

## Stability matrix

| Dimension | Stable reading | Failure reading |
|---|---|---|
| Invariant | relation survives carrier change | slogan survives, function disappears |
| Transmission | members can reconstruct enough to act | archive exists but no living uptake |
| Variation | mutation remains recognizable as related | mutation is treated as betrayal by default |
| Profession | specialist judgment adds value | profession colonizes ordinary life |
| Boundary | difference has a cost and a route | boundary is either invisible or absolute |
| Recognition | members can detect the operating move | authority declares recognition unilaterally |
| Repair | violation produces a workable response | rupture is hidden or ritualized away |
| Counter-move | subordinate can reverse or refuse | only dominant actor has speech |
| Translation | function transfers with marked loss | surface is copied and function is lost |
| History | ancestry informs but does not dictate | retrospective naming becomes destiny |

## Adversarial cases

Use these as fixtures, not as automatic conclusions:

1. same invariant, different carrier;
2. same surface, different function;
3. different surface, same function;
4. clade recognizes a member that the world rejects;
5. world recognizes a member that the clade has expelled;
6. outsider performs the custom more accurately than a member;
7. imposed rule later becomes genuinely useful;
8. inherited obligation is refused without destroying continuity;
9. professional caste claims to embody the whole Kultur;
10. archive preserves a dead custom as if alive;
11. a taboo prevents harm but also prevents repair;
12. a counter-move changes position without changing the rule;
13. translation preserves surface but loses recognition;
14. translation loses surface but preserves function;
15. hybrid body carries incompatible kernels;
16. a faction invents ancestry to legitimate a present decision.

Score relations, not labels:

- invariant preserved;
- carrier changed;
- recognition state;
- response available;
- positional consequence;
- repair achieved or failed;
- alternative explanation preserved.

## Provisional names, Kultur continuity, and adversarial dialogue

> **Insertion note — 2026-11-02 14:58 EST (America/Toronto).** Conversation capture added as a working extension after the recent Die Kultur/Kultur research. This note does not change this skill's TEST-READY / NOT CANON status, promote a hypothesis, or make the examples canon.

### Name the unknown without deciding its cause

A provisional name can make a pattern available for comparison, manipulation and boundary testing before its ontology or cause is known. It is a handle, not proof. Röntgen's “X” is the model: preserve the observed phenomenon as an object of inquiry while withholding a causal story that could redirect or prematurely close the investigation.

Keep separate:

- observed phenomenon;
- provisional name;
- proposed explanation;
- evidence that would distinguish explanations;
- open alternatives.

A name may be wrong and still be useful if its provisional status remains visible and it helps ask better questions. It becomes a failure when the label hardens into an assumed entity or imports a causal account the evidence has not earned.

### Distinguish recurring form from transmitted Kultur

When a structure or capability appears in separate lineages, specify what recurs: visible form, function, underlying problem, capability, social role, or a particular historical event. Similarity under shared constraints does not establish common ancestry, identical function, or continuity of Kultur. Preserve the clade and the path by which each instance arose.

An enduring artifact can outlast its makers without carrying forward the living knowledge that made it. A pyramid-like form may recur for different reasons; that does not imply the recurrence of the political, ethical, technical or intergenerational system that produced a specific civilization. Conversely, complex infrastructure and technologies depend on particular chains of material access, accumulated practice, instruments, institutions and transmission. Their recovery cannot be inferred from the recurrence of simpler forms.

Use the distinction:

`artifact persists ≠ usable knowledge transfers ≠ Kultur continues`

The archive may retain an object or slogan after its function has died. Test whether later members can reconstruct and use the relation, teach it onward, repair it under pressure, and recognize when the surface remains but the function has changed. Claims that a technological window is unique or will close remain risk hypotheses until supported independently.

### Put an idea under pressure from several sides

A research or editorial conversation can work as a noncompetitive sequence: expose a candidate idea; attack it; examine and attack the critique; defend or revise the idea from another angle; then keep the unresolved vectors visible. The purpose is to reveal load-bearing assumptions and possibilities, not to win or force a synthesis.

Do not treat each user correction as a request for a smoother restatement. If a correction shows that the current model is wrong, change the model and state what distinction now governs the work. Delay synthesis while competing explanations still change the decision. Keep evidence, inference, user correction and speculation distinguishable.

### Test prompts

- What exactly recurs, and what evidence supports that level of recurrence?
- Does the carrier survive while its function, knowledge or obligation disappears?
- What can the next generation actually reconstruct and transmit?
- Does the provisional name keep the cause open, or smuggle one in?
- Which attack changed the model, and which disagreement remains unresolved?

### Review 0.1.1 — 2026-11-02 15:16 EST (America/Toronto)

This revision makes three distinct relations explicit. It preserves the skill's existing TEST-READY / NOT CANON status and does not promote a Kultur or a historical hypothesis.

**Recurrence is not Kultur continuity.** Under shared material or environmental constraints, a visible form, function, problem-solving capacity or social role may emerge independently. Similarity alone does not establish common ancestry or a shared Kultur. When a lineage or transfer is claimed, identify its carrier and transmission path; when that path is unknown, retain convergence and other explanations as live alternatives.

**Artifact persistence is not living transmission.** A monument, archive, slogan or tool can outlast its makers while the practical knowledge and obligations that gave it function disappear. Call Kultur continuity only where members can reconstruct and use the relevant relations, transmit them to others, and renew or repair them under pressure. A copied surface or durable remnant is not enough.

**A recurring capacity does not replace a singular history.** Distinguish the recurrence of a capability or role from the exact person, event, clade and path that realized it. Keep the clade when comparing convergent outcomes. Claims that a technological window is unique or irrecoverable remain hypotheses unless supported by evidence beyond the analogy.

Apply these distinctions in construction, recognition, transmission and pressure tests. Do not merge form, function, lineage and Kultur into one continuity score.

## Failure boundaries

- Do not derive a Kultur from phenotype, species label or aesthetic mood.
- Do not confuse shared capability with shared personality.
- Do not treat ancestry as proof of obligation.
- Do not make every disagreement a separate clade.
- Do not make every clade variation a harmless style.
- Do not reward a culture for being internally consistent if it cannot repair
  contradiction.
- Do not call a custom stable because an authority says it is stable.
- Do not treat recognition as binary when target, witness, reader and
  institution occupy different states.
- Do not use Albur as a synonym for any ambiguity, joke or hidden meaning.
- Do not promote a generated Kultur to world canon without a pressure test.

## Minimum safe test

Input:

- one world;
- two related clades;
- one shared inherited practice;
- one environmental pressure;
- one outsider description;
- one member who refuses the expected role.

Produce:

1. a three-to-seven-invariant kernel;
2. common competence versus specialist judgment;
3. recognition topology;
4. faithful, mutated, counterfeit and reversed variants;
5. a pressure comparison showing what remained functional;
6. one repair and one unresolved fracture;
7. a receipt stating which relations are proven in the fixture and which are
   only world hypotheses.

## Promotion gate

Promote only when the test demonstrates that the Kultur changes construction
decisions. At minimum it must alter one of:

- how a member acts;
- how a clade recognizes membership;
- how a profession is separated from common competence;
- how a violation is repaired;
- how a translated or mutated form remains related;
- how an outsider's description is exposed as incomplete or imposed.

If the system merely produces attractive customs, retain it as seed material.
