# Albur / Calambre Experimental Cage

Status: experimental; not canon. Opening signature: Muse.

## Isolation contract
Treat this file and the candidate supplied in the current task as the complete test environment. Do not import Genesis Seed, prior conversations, Library, cultural taxonomies, or a preferred mature theory unless the user explicitly supplies them. General language competence may be used to parse the candidate, but it must not silently fill missing evidence.

The suite holds multiple hypotheses available but does not activate them all simultaneously. Select one primary configuration that best tests the candidate. Activate secondary configurations only when a concrete feature requires them. Do not combine incompatible hypotheses merely to save a classification.

## Working definitions
Albur: candidate family of verbal or symbolic practices in which a construction exposes one or more participants to a risk of loss, especially position, dignity, interpretive control, or ability to answer. Sexuality, double meaning, improvisation, duel, humiliation and calambre may participate; none is assumed individually necessary.

Candidate membership condition: if there is no meaningful possibility of losing, classification as albur must be challenged rather than presumed.

Calambre: broader unresolved phenomenon. It may be a functional quality, experienced effect, relational perturbation, positional consequence, or another still-unresolved category. Do not force one ontology.

Calambur: a possible form/operation, not automatically albur.
Displacement: a possible operation that can expose another reading while leaving the first plane available; not automatically albur or calambre.

Keep intention, construction, reception and consequence separate.

## Available configurations — select, do not stack by default
A. Sexual duel: reciprocal sexual double meaning; recognition, response timing, reversal and defeat matter.
B. Nonsexual humiliation: stupidity, incompetence, subordination or ridicule without requiring sexuality; distinguish from direct insult.
C. Albur with calambre: candidate move plus observed perturbation; distinguish intended from produced effect.
D. Albur without observed calambre: risk structure exists but perturbation is absent or unobserved.
E. Asymmetric adversary: participant faces audience, institution, social order or universe that need not risk losing; success may be only avoiding humiliation.
F. Self-targeting: emitter and target overlap; test self-exposure, self-mockery and reader position.
G. Literary albur-device: oral duel logic survives through writing, delayed recognition, absent opponent, reader inversion, composition or translation.
H. Calambre without albur: threat, insinuation, silence, private knowledge or another intervention perturbs without meeting candidate albur conditions.
I. Displacement without albur/calambre: reading, segmentation, perceptual order or interpretation changes without interpersonal wager.
J. Institutional frontier: play for one participant can become harassment or verbal violence for another; inspect power, consent, exit and consequences.
K. Reversibility: recipient turns the received construction back; distinguish a new counter-move from appropriation of the original weapon.
L. Distributed recognition: emitter, target, witnesses and reader can recognize different planes at different times.
M. Functional translation: test what survives when phonology, segmentation, sexuality or literal content cannot.
N. Conventionalization: recognizability can rise while novelty, difficulty or prestige falls; comprehension is not competence.

## Candidate formulas
Formal: `S --(O,K)--> {P1,P2}` where S=surface, O=operation, K=context/code, P1/P2=possible readings.
Interaction: `move -> recognition -> response / evasion / silence / reversal`; any transition may fail or be absent.
Risk screen: `albur? = situated construction + exposure to loss + possibility of resolution`. This is a hypothesis, not a proven sufficient condition.
Calambre: `intervention + reception + situation -> possible effect`; do not define the effect in advance.
Asymmetric: `participant <-> non-defeatable universe`; participant may lose or save position while the universe risks nothing.

## Test protocol
1. Describe only the supplied candidate and evidence.
2. Select one primary configuration; state why. Add secondary configurations only if required.
3. Separate surface, operation, practice, interpretation, response, risk and effect.
4. Identify who can lose, what can be lost, and under what condition. Separate prior risk from actual loss.
5. Identify who recognizes each reading; do not invent shared recognition.
6. Test reversal, refusal, non-recognition and exit when applicable.
7. Construct the smallest counterexample or mutation that would break the proposed classification.
8. Report uncertainty and competing classifications without merging them.
9. Extract only the delta that the candidate contributes to the working definition. Do not rewrite a history of the theory.

## Required output
CASE — compact description.
EVIDENCE — directly supplied/observable.
PRIMARY CONFIGURATION — one, with reason.
SECONDARY — only if necessary.
OPERATION — what materially happens.
RISK — who can lose what and how.
RECEPTION — who understands/responds/ignores/misreads.
RESULT — loss, save, reversal, indeterminate, or other.
CALAMBRE — present / absent / indeterminate, with evidence and no forced ontology.
PROVISIONAL CLASSIFICATION — albur / albur-device / neighboring mechanism / not albur / indeterminate.
COUNTEREXAMPLE — minimum mutation that breaks the classification.
DELTA — only the new observation that should modify the working definition, if any.

## Adversarial fixtures available when the user asks to exercise the cage itself
Calambur with no risk; direct humiliating insult; sexual albur without response; nonsexual albur with humiliation; risk without damage; returned attack; target misses while audience recognizes; reader-before-character; character-before-reader; participant versus non-losing adversary; literary self-mockery; threatening insinuation without albur; visual displacement without interaction; conventionalized construction; translation preserving function but losing form; peer duel entering an institution.

## Failure conditions
Fail the run if it: classifies from aggression/tone alone; treats double meaning, sexuality, calambur or humiliation as sufficient; equates calambre with albur; invents intention/reception/damage/risk; activates every configuration at once; imports outside project context; or converts one successful hypothesis into universal canon.

Success means the run preserves distinctions between mechanism, practice, risk, reception and effect even when one candidate contains several of them.
